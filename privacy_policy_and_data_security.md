**Privacy Policy for Pet Encyclopedia (宠物大百科)**

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how "Pet Encyclopedia" / "宠物大百科" (the "App"), package name `com.petpedia.app`, handles information when you use it.

**1. Information We Collect**
The App **does not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to our servers or any third parties. Breed encyclopedia text, diet and disease guides, classroom articles, food checker, symptom checker, and first-aid content are bundled in the App and processed on your device.

If you choose to use optional features, the following stays **only on your device**:
*   Pet profiles you enter (such as name, species, breed, birthday, sex, weight, clinic, and notes)
*   Vaccine and care calendar events
*   Favorite breeds
*   Language and theme preferences

We do not require an account, email, or phone number.

**2. Permissions Required**
The App does **not** request Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or nearby-devices permissions. It is designed to work fully offline.

**3. Data Storage**
Optional profiles, calendar events, favorites, language, and theme are saved locally using standard Android storage (SharedPreferences). They are never uploaded or shared by the App.

You can delete this data at any time by:
*   deleting a pet profile inside the App, or
*   clearing the App's data in Android Settings, or
*   uninstalling the App.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, the system may include this local App data in your device backup. That backup is controlled by your Google / device account settings, not by our servers.

**4. External Links & Third Parties**
The App does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social-login SDKs that track your behavior. Encyclopedia content is for husbandry education only and is not a veterinary diagnosis.

**5. Children's Privacy**
The App is a general pet-care reference. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any names or notes you type into a pet profile remain on the device.

**6. Changes to This Privacy Policy**
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

**7. Contact Us**
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at [Insert Your Support Email Here].

**《宠物大百科》隐私政策**

**生效日期：** 2026年8月18日

我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明“宠物大百科”（英文名 Pet Encyclopedia，应用包名 `com.petpedia.app`，以下简称“本应用”）在您使用过程中如何处理相关信息。
